MATERI PERTEMUAN 35: Kosakata Akademik Lingkungan

Term Definition
Biodiversity The variety of living things (plants, animals, and insects) in one place.
Sustainability Using resources (water, energy, materials) without causing long-term damage.
Ecosystem A community of living things and the place where they live, working together.
Pollution Dirty or harmful things in the air, water, or land.
Awareness Knowing about and understanding a situation or problem.

MATERI PERTEMUAN 36: Kalimat Kompleks Dasar

Conjunction Function Example
Because Gives a reason. Plastic waste is dangerous because it takes hundreds of years to decompose.
So Shows a result. The canteen uses a lot of plastic, so the school produces a lot of waste.
Although Shows a contrast. Although recycling bins are available, many students still throw plastic in the wrong bin.

MATERI PERTEMUAN 37-38: Struktur Generik dan Model Text

A. Generic Structure of Analytical Exposition

Structure Function Typical Language Signal
Thesis States the writer's position or opinion about an issue clearly at the beginning. I believe that... / It is important to realise that...
Arguments Presents reasons, data, or facts that support the thesis. There is usually more than one argument. Firstly... / Secondly... / Furthermore... / According to...
Reiteration Restates the thesis to close the text, often with a call to action. In conclusion... / Therefore, we must...

B. Model Text: "Why Our School Must Reduce Plastic Waste"

Thesis: I believe that our school must take serious action to reduce plastic waste. Every day, the school canteen and classrooms produce a large amount of plastic waste that harms both our local environment and the wider ecosystem.

Arguments: Firstly, according to a survey conducted by the student council, the canteen produces more than two hundred plastic bottles and wrappers every single day. This waste often ends up in nearby drains, which eventually connect to the sea, threatening marine biodiversity. Secondly, plastic waste takes hundreds of years to decompose, so it will remain in our environment long after we have graduated. Although the school has provided several recycling bins, many students still throw plastic waste into the wrong bin, showing that awareness alone is not enough without a clear system and consistent habits.

Reiteration: In conclusion, our school must reduce plastic waste through clear rules, better recycling systems, and consistent habits from every student. Protecting our school's ecosystem today means protecting a more sustainable future for everyone.

LAMPIRAN 6 - MATERI AJAR (IN ENGLISH, LENGKAP DAN RINCI PER PERTEMUAN)

MATERI PERTEMUAN 39: Simple Present Tense

We use the simple present tense in analytical exposition texts to state facts and general truths, not events that happened at a specific time in the past. This is different from the simple past tense used in Unit 1 and Unit 2.

Subject Verb Form Example
I / You / We / They Base form (no change) Students throw plastic waste every day.
He / She / It Base form + s/es The canteen produces plastic waste every day.

MATERI PERTEMUAN 40: Causal Conjunctions

Conjunction Position in Sentence Example
Thus Usually starts a new sentence, after a full stop. Plastic waste harms marine life. Thus, our school must take action.
Therefore Usually starts a new sentence, like thus. Slightly more formal. The canteen produces too much waste. Therefore, a new policy is needed.
Due to Followed by a noun phrase, not a full clause. Due to plastic pollution, marine biodiversity is declining.

Note: because is followed by a full clause (subject + verb), while due to is followed by a noun phrase only. Compare: "...because the canteen uses too much plastic" (clause) versus "...due to too much plastic use" (noun phrase).

MATERI PERTEMUAN 41-42: Claim-Evidence-Reasoning

A. What Is Claim-Evidence-Reasoning?

Part Function Example
Claim A clear statement of what you believe or argue. Our school should reduce plastic waste.
Evidence Real data or facts that support the claim. The canteen produces more than 200 plastic items per day (student council survey).
Reasoning Explains why the evidence matters and how it supports the claim. This means the school produces a lot of waste that better systems could easily reduce.

B. Sample Argument (Model)

Claim: Our school should install more water refill stations. Evidence: According to a survey by the student council, students buy an average of 150 plastic water bottles per day. Reasoning: Therefore, more refill stations would directly reduce this daily plastic waste. Students could fill their own bottles instead of buying new ones.

PERTEMUAN 51 (2 JP / 60 MENIT) Rekap dan Verifikasi Kelengkapan Pengumpulan Tugas
Kegiatan Awal (10 Menit)
Prinsip: Berkesadaran

1. Guru mengucapkan salam pembuka: "Good morning, everyone. Today, we finish reviewing the amazing work you created during Ramadan." Memimpin doa, memeriksa kehadiran.

2. Guru menjelaskan bahwa Unit 3 dinilai sepenuhnya dari produk (teks dan video), tanpa ulangan tertulis, mengingat kondisi bulan Ramadan.

3. Guru menyampaikan tujuan pembelajaran: memverifikasi kelengkapan seluruh produk kelompok dan menuntaskan penilaian rubrik.
Kegiatan Inti (2 JP x 30 Menit = 45 Menit)

MENGAPLIKASIKAN (Verifikasi dan Penilaian Produk) - 45 Menit

1. Guru merekap dan memverifikasi kelengkapan pengumpulan tugas seluruh kelompok (teks formal dan video kampanye).

2. Guru menuntaskan penilaian rubrik produk yang belum selesai dinilai saat pemutaran Pertemuan 50.

3. Bagi kelompok yang belum mengumpulkan, guru memberikan batas waktu tambahan yang jelas.

4. Sambil menunggu giliran verifikasi, setiap kelompok mengecek sendiri kelengkapan produknya dengan daftar cek rubrik. (Kemandirian)
Kegiatan Penutup (5 Menit)
Prinsip: Berkesadaran, Bermakna

1. Guru menyampaikan apresiasi atas usaha kelompok menyelesaikan proyek di tengah kondisi Ramadan.

2. Guru menyampaikan rencana Pertemuan 52: umpan balik tertulis dan kesempatan revisi.

3. Guru menutup pembelajaran dengan doa dan salam.
PERTEMUAN 52 (3 JP / 90 MENIT) Umpan Balik Tertulis dan Kesempatan Revisi Mandiri
Kegiatan Awal (10 Menit)
Prinsip: Berkesadaran, Menggembirakan

1. Guru mengucapkan salam pembuka, memimpin doa, memeriksa kehadiran.

2. Guru menyampaikan gambaran umum kualitas produk secara positif, menyoroti kekuatan yang sering muncul dan area yang masih perlu diperkuat.

3. Guru menyampaikan tujuan pembelajaran hari ini: menerima umpan balik tertulis dan memanfaatkan kesempatan revisi bila diperlukan.
Kegiatan Inti (3 JP x 30 Menit = 80 Menit)

MEMAHAMI (Umpan Balik Tertulis) - 25 Menit

1. Guru membagikan umpan balik tertulis singkat ke tiap kelompok, mencakup kekuatan dan area perbaikan produk mereka.

2. Kelompok membaca dan mendiskusikan umpan balik yang diterima.

MENGAPLIKASIKAN (Revisi Mandiri) - 35 Menit

1. Kelompok yang produknya belum memenuhi rubrik minimal (skor di bawah 75) melakukan revisi mandiri berdasarkan umpan balik guru.

2. Kelompok yang sudah tuntas memperbaiki satu bagian produk yang paling lemah menurut umpan balik sebagai pengayaan. (Kreativitas)

3. Guru berkeliling memberi bimbingan tambahan bagi kelompok yang melakukan revisi.

MEREFLEKSI (Refleksi Diri dan Rekap Nilai) - 20 Menit

1. Setiap murid menulis refleksi singkat tentang pengalaman mengerjakan proyek selama Ramadan: satu hal yang sudah ia kuasai, satu hal yang masih perlu dilatih, dan satu rencana belajar untuk Unit 4. (Kemandirian, Penalaran Kritis)

2. Guru merekap nilai akhir Unit 3 (gabungan rubrik produk dari Pertemuan 45-50 dan hasil revisi bila ada).
Kegiatan Penutup (5 Menit)
Prinsip: Bermakna, Menggembirakan

1. Murid bersama Guru menyimpulkan capaian keseluruhan Unit 3: dari memahami struktur eksposisi analitis hingga mencipta kampanye lingkungan nyata.

2. Guru memberikan apresiasi menyeluruh atas kemandirian luar biasa yang ditunjukkan murid selama proyek berlangsung di bulan Ramadan.

3. Guru menyampaikan pengantar Unit 4 (Hortatory Exposition, tema gaya hidup sehat) yang akan dimulai pada pertemuan berikutnya.

4. Guru menutup pembelajaran dengan doa dan salam: "Well done, everyone! Your voices for the environment truly matter."
