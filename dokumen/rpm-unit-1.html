B. IDENTIFIKASI
Identifikasi Murid

Pengetahuan Awal: Murid telah memiliki pengalaman mendengarkan cerita rakyat/legenda dalam Bahasa Indonesia pada jenjang pendidikan sebelumnya, namun belum terbiasa mengidentifikasi struktur generik, ciri genre, kosakata tematik, maupun ide pokok teks naratif berbahasa Inggris secara eksplisit.

Minat Belajar: Murid Kelas XI pada umumnya menyukai kegiatan bercerita, menyimak audio, permainan tebak-gambar, dan diskusi kelompok kecil dibandingkan metode ceramah satu arah; sebagian murid menunjukkan ketertarikan terhadap isu lingkungan hidup dan cerita rakyat daerah asal melalui media sosial.

Kebutuhan Belajar: Dari 44 murid dalam satu rombongan belajar, hanya sekitar 10 murid yang mahir berbahasa Inggris; sebagian besar murid memerlukan penguatan kosakata dan tata bahasa dasar, disertai dukungan visual, audio, dan aktivitas gerak (kinestetik) untuk mengakomodasi keragaman gaya belajar di kelas.
Materi Pelajaran

Faktual: Teks “The Legend of the Weeping Lake” (lisan pada Pertemuan 1, tertulis pada Pertemuan 3); legenda Pulau Setokok (Batam); legenda daerah asal siswa (PR Pertemuan 2); teks “The Legend of Telaga Lestari” (Pertemuan 4).

Konseptual: Struktur generik teks naratif (orientation, complication, resolution); ciri-ciri umum legenda; kosakata tematik lingkungan (ecosystem, habitat, deforestation, extinction, conservation); konsep ide pokok paragraf.

Prosedural: Langkah mengidentifikasi struktur generik melalui anotasi; langkah Text Evidence Matching untuk memaknai kosakata baru; langkah note-taking “3 magic questions” untuk menemukan ide pokok.

Metakognitif: Kesadaran murid dalam memantau strategi menyimak, mencari bukti tekstual, dan menyimpulkan ide pokok yang paling efektif bagi dirinya sendiri.
Dimensi Profil Lulusan (DPL)

Penalaran Kritis (utama): Murid mengidentifikasi struktur, ciri genre, makna kosakata, dan ide pokok secara logis berdasarkan bukti tekstual.

Komunikasi (penunjang): Murid menyampaikan hasil temuan secara lisan dan tertulis dengan jelas.

Kemandirian (penunjang): Murid membangun kepercayaan diri mengerjakan identifikasi secara mandiri sebelum verifikasi berpasangan/kelompok.

Kolaborasi (penunjang, Pertemuan 2 dan 4): Murid bekerja sama dalam kelompok kecil untuk menyusun dan memverifikasi temuan.
C. DESAIN PEMBELAJARAN
Capaian Pembelajaran Pada akhir Fase F, murid memahami alur cerita atau informasi dari teks narasi tentang isu kesadaran lingkungan secara lisan, tulis, atau multimodal; mengidentifikasi struktur generik dan unsur kebahasaan teks; mengevaluasi dan merefleksi informasi tersurat maupun tersirat; serta mengomunikasikan gagasan melalui penulisan cerita pendek dengan struktur teks dan unsur kebahasaan yang tepat, jelas, dan detail, sesuai kompetensi CEFR Level B2.
Lintas Disiplin Ilmu

Geografi/IPS: keterkaitan isu ekosistem dan lingkungan yang menjadi latar cerita legenda.

Bahasa Indonesia: kesamaan struktur teks naratif/legenda dengan cerita rakyat Nusantara sebagai jembatan pemahaman lintas bahasa.
Tujuan Pembelajaran TP 1.1 Peserta didik dapat mengidentifikasi (C1) struktur generik (orientation-complication-resolution) dan ciri-ciri teks legenda bertema lingkungan (unsur ajaib, fungsi etiologis, pesan moral, latar nyata, tradisi lisan), serta memahami (C2) kosakata tematik lingkungan dan ide pokok tiap bagian struktur melalui teknik note-taking, berdasarkan teks “The Legend of the Weeping Lake” dan legenda lingkungan dari daerah asal siswa sendiri (mis. Batam).
Topik Pembelajaran Struktur generik teks naratif; ciri-ciri legenda; kosakata tematik lingkungan; ide pokok paragraf melalui note-taking; teks lisan/tertulis bertema lingkungan.
Praktik Pedagogis

Pendekatan: Pembelajaran Mendalam (Deep Learning): Memahami → Mengaplikasikan → Merefleksi (PPA Edisi Revisi 2025).

Model (Pertemuan 1-4): Discovery Learning.

Sintaks: Stimulation (Pemberian Rangsangan) → Problem Statement (Identifikasi Masalah) → Data Collection (Pengumpulan Data) → Data Processing (Pengolahan Data) → Verification (Pembuktian) → Generalization (Menarik Kesimpulan).

Metode: Menyimak terbimbing (guided listening), diskusi kelompok kecil, Text Evidence Matching, note-taking terbimbing, tanya jawab, dan penugasan individu dengan verifikasi berpasangan/kelompok.
Kemitraan Pembelajaran

Pertemuan 1 dan 3: Kemitraan berpasangan (pair verification) untuk memeriksa hasil identifikasi individu.

Pertemuan 2 dan 4: Kemitraan kelompok kecil (tiga sampai empat orang) untuk menyusun dan memverifikasi temuan secara kolaboratif.
Lingkungan Pembelajaran Kelas ditata membentuk kelompok kecil menghadap papan tulis dan sumber audio; suasana kelas menyerupai “listening circle” yang tenang namun interaktif. Budaya kelas: “Listen closely, discover the pattern.” Ramah bagi murid dengan kemampuan Bahasa Inggris yang beragam.

PERTEMUAN 1 (3 JP / 90 MENIT) Story Detectives - Struktur Generik Teks Legenda
Kegiatan Awal (10 Menit)
Prinsip: Berkesadaran, Bermakna

1. Guru menampilkan gambar pemantik (danau/hutan misterius) sejak sebelum bel berbunyi, membiarkan murid menebak-nebak sejenak.

2. Guru mengucapkan salam pembuka: “Good morning, everyone! Today we start our journey as story detectives.” Memimpin doa dan memeriksa kehadiran.

3. Guru mengajukan pertanyaan pemantik: “Have you ever heard a legend about how a place, mountain, or animal came to be? What made the story memorable for you?”

4. Guru mengaitkan jawaban murid (mis. Malin Kundang, Tangkuban Perahu, atau legenda Pulau Setokok, Batam) dengan tujuan pembelajaran hari ini: mengidentifikasi struktur generik teks legenda bertema lingkungan yang didengar.
Kegiatan Inti (2 JP × 30 Menit = 60 Menit)

MEMAHAMI (Stimulation & Problem Statement) - 25 Menit

1. Guru menampilkan ilustrasi legenda lingkungan (danau dikelilingi hutan) dan meminta murid memprediksi isi cerita secara lisan.

2. Guru memutar rekaman audio “The Legend of the Weeping Lake” untuk pertama kali; murid menyimak tanpa mencatat.

3. Guru mengajukan pertanyaan pemantik: “What do you think happens first, in the middle, and at the end of this story?”

4. Murid secara individu menuliskan dugaan awal (orientation, complication, resolution) pada LKPD Part A. (Penalaran Kritis)

MENGAPLIKASIKAN (Data Collection & Data Processing) - 30 Menit

1. Guru memutar ulang rekaman secara bertahap per bagian; murid mencatat pada tabel anotasi LKPD Part B.

2. Murid menandai orientation, complication, dan resolution berdasarkan bukti yang didengar, bekerja individu terlebih dahulu. (Kemandirian, Penalaran Kritis)

3. Murid berpasangan membandingkan dan memverifikasi hasil identifikasi, menuliskan kesepakatan akhir.

4. Guru berkeliling membimbing pasangan yang kesulitan membedakan complication dan resolution.

5. Guru membacakan ringkasan singkat legenda kedua yang belum pernah didengar murid (2-3 kalimat, misalnya legenda daerah di sekitar Batam). Pasangan menentukan secara lisan bagian orientation, complication, dan resolution tanpa catatan, lalu menyebutkan kata atau kalimat yang menjadi buktinya. (Penalaran Kritis)

MEREFLEKSI (Verification & Generalization) - 5 Menit

1. Beberapa pasangan mempresentasikan singkat hasil identifikasi mereka.

2. Guru dan murid mencocokkan hasil dengan struktur generik yang sebenarnya (verification).

3. Murid menyimpulkan ciri khas tiap bagian struktur generik (generalization) melalui diskusi kelas.

4. Murid menuliskan satu kalimat refleksi pada LKPD Part C tentang bagian struktur yang paling mudah/sulit diidentifikasi. (Penalaran Kritis)
Kegiatan Penutup (20 Menit)
Prinsip: Bermakna, Menggembirakan

1. Murid bersama Guru menyimpulkan pembelajaran: “Today we learned to identify the beginning, problem, and solution of a legend.”

2. Guru memberikan umpan balik terhadap partisipasi murid selama menyimak dan diskusi.

3. Guru menyampaikan rencana Pertemuan 2 dan arahan tugas (PR): murid diminta membaca kembali catatan hasil identifikasi struktur sebagai bekal Pertemuan 2.

4. Guru menutup pembelajaran dengan doa dan salam: “Great job today, story detectives! See you next lesson.”
PERTEMUAN 2 (2 JP / 60 MENIT) Legend Hunters - Ciri-Ciri Legenda Bertema Lingkungan
Kegiatan Awal (7 Menit)
Prinsip: Berkesadaran, Menggembirakan

1. Guru mengucapkan salam pembuka: “Good morning, everyone! Ready to become legend experts today?” Memimpin doa, memeriksa kehadiran.

2. Guru mengajukan pertanyaan pemantik: “Besides the structure we found yesterday, what makes a story feel like a 'legend' rather than just any story?”

3. Guru menyampaikan tujuan pembelajaran hari ini: mengidentifikasi ciri-ciri khas legenda bertema lingkungan.
Kegiatan Inti (2 JP × 30 Menit = 45 Menit)

MEMAHAMI (Stimulation & Problem Statement) - 12 Menit

1. Guru menampilkan dua teks pendek berdampingan (satu legenda, satu berita lingkungan); murid membandingkan sekilas.

2. Guru bertanya: “What makes the first text feel different from the second one?”

3. Murid berkelompok (3-4 orang) mendiskusikan dugaan awal ciri-ciri legenda (tokoh gaib, asal-usul tempat, pesan moral). (Kolaborasi)

4. Guru mencatat dugaan murid di papan tulis sebagai peta konsep awal.

MENGAPLIKASIKAN (Data Collection & Data Processing) - 25 Menit

1. Tiap kelompok menerima potongan teks legenda berbeda dan mengisi checklist 5 ciri legenda pada LKPD.

2. Murid mendiskusikan dan mencatat bukti tekstual pendukung tiap ciri (unsur ajaib, latar nyata, pesan moral, dsb.), menerapkan teknik Round Robin. (Penalaran Kritis, Kemandirian)

3. Guru berkeliling memfasilitasi diskusi dan memberi pertanyaan pemandu bagi kelompok yang kesulitan.

4. Tiap kelompok menyusun daftar akhir ciri-ciri legenda.

MEREFLEKSI (Verification & Generalization) - 8 Menit

1. Perwakilan kelompok memaparkan singkat ciri-ciri yang ditemukan.

2. Guru dan murid mencocokkan temuan dengan rujukan resmi 5 ciri legenda (verification).

3. Murid menyimpulkan definisi dan ciri khas legenda bertema lingkungan (generalization).

4. Murid menuliskan refleksi singkat kontribusinya dalam diskusi kelompok. (Kolaborasi, Penalaran Kritis)
Kegiatan Penutup (8 Menit)
Prinsip: Bermakna, Menggembirakan

1. Murid bersama Guru menyimpulkan struktur generik dan ciri-ciri legenda bertema lingkungan.

2. Guru memberikan umpan balik terhadap hasil kerja kelompok dan mengapresiasi partisipasi murid.

3. Guru menyampaikan arahan tugas (PR): murid mencari satu contoh legenda daerah asalnya masing-masing yang berkaitan dengan lingkungan (boleh berbahasa Indonesia) untuk dibahas ringkas pada Pertemuan 3 dan 4.

4. Guru menutup pembelajaran dengan doa dan salam: “Wonderful teamwork today! See you at our next lesson.”
PERTEMUAN 3 (3 JP / 90 MENIT) Word Explorers - Kosakata Tematik Lingkungan (Text Evidence Matching)
Kegiatan Awal (10 Menit)
Prinsip: Berkesadaran, Bermakna

1. Guru menampilkan foto deforestasi/hutan gundul sejak murid masuk kelas.

2. Guru mengucapkan salam pembuka: “Good morning, everyone! Ready to grow your environmental vocabulary today?” Memimpin doa, memeriksa kehadiran.

3. Guru mengajak murid me-recall pertemuan sebelumnya (struktur dan ciri legenda), lalu bertanya: “Do you know what words like 'ecosystem' or 'deforestation' really mean, and why they matter to our story?”

4. Guru menyampaikan tujuan pembelajaran: mempelajari lima kosakata tematik lingkungan dan strategi menemukan bukti makna kata dalam teks (Text Evidence Matching).
Kegiatan Inti (3 JP × 30 Menit = 80 Menit)

MEMAHAMI (Stimulation & Problem Statement) - 25 Menit

1. Guru menampilkan galeri 5 gambar tanpa label (ecosystem, habitat, deforestation, extinction, conservation); murid menebak kata untuk tiap gambar.

2. Guru mengonfirmasi label dan definisi singkat tiap kata bersama murid (choral response).

3. Guru membacakan teks lengkap “The Legend of the Weeping Lake” (murid sudah mendengarnya di Pertemuan 1, kini pertama kali melihatnya tertulis).

4. Guru mencontohkan strategi Text Evidence Matching: menemukan kalimat bukti untuk kata “deforestation” meski kata itu sendiri tidak tertulis literal di teks (“They wanted to cut down the entire forest to sell the wood.”).

MENGAPLIKASIKAN (Data Collection & Data Processing) - 45 Menit

1. Murid secara individu mencari kalimat bukti untuk 4 kata tersisa (ecosystem, habitat, extinction, conservation) pada LKPD.

2. Khusus kata “extinction”, karena tidak ada bukti langsung di teks, murid menjawab pertanyaan berpikir kritis: mengapa hewan hutan bisa terancam extinction jika kejadian seperti ini terjadi berulang-ulang. (Penalaran Kritis)

3. Murid berpasangan membandingkan kalimat bukti dan mendiskusikan perbedaan.

4. Murid menulis satu kalimat sendiri memakai minimal 2 kosakata baru. Guru berkeliling membimbing, terutama untuk pertanyaan kritis “extinction”.

MEREFLEKSI (Verification & Generalization) - 10 Menit

1. Beberapa pasangan membacakan hasil pencarian bukti mereka.

2. Guru dan murid mencocokkan dengan kunci jawaban resmi serta mendiskusikan poin kritis “extinction” bersama.

3. Murid menyimpulkan strategi Text Evidence Matching sebagai cara memaknai kata tanpa perlu kata itu tertulis literal.

4. Murid menuliskan refleksi singkat pada LKPD.
Kegiatan Penutup (10 Menit)
Prinsip: Bermakna, Menggembirakan

1. Murid bersama Guru menyimpulkan lima kosakata tematik lingkungan dan strategi Text Evidence Matching.

2. Guru memberikan umpan balik terhadap hasil kerja murid.

3. Guru menyampaikan rencana Pertemuan 4: menemukan ide pokok menggunakan teknik note-taking.

4. Guru menutup pembelajaran dengan doa dan salam: “Great job today, word explorers!”
PERTEMUAN 4 (2 JP / 60 MENIT) Idea Hunters - Menemukan Ide Pokok melalui Note-Taking
Kegiatan Awal (5 Menit)
Prinsip: Berkesadaran, Menggembirakan

1. Guru menampilkan kalimat pemantik dari legenda baru (“...the lake has been called Telaga Lestari, meaning a place that remains preserved.”) sejak murid masuk kelas, belum dijelaskan.

2. Guru mengucapkan salam pembuka: “Good morning, everyone! Today we become idea hunters!” Memimpin doa, memeriksa kehadiran.

3. Guru mengajak recall kosakata Pertemuan 3 dikaitkan dengan cerita Weeping Lake.

4. Guru menyampaikan dua tujuan pembelajaran hari ini: menemukan ide pokok paragraf, dan menggunakan teknik note-taking.
Kegiatan Inti (2 JP × 30 Menit = 50 Menit)

MEMAHAMI (Stimulation & Problem Statement) - 10 Menit

1. Guru mengajarkan strategi “3 Magic Questions”: (1) WHO/WHAT is this paragraph about? (2) WHAT HAPPENED/WHAT DID THEY DO? (3) WHAT WAS THE RESULT?

2. Guru mencontohkan pada paragraf Orientation “Mai Rimba” (cerita yang sudah familiar) dengan kata kunci digarisbawahi, lalu menggabungkan tiga jawaban menjadi satu kalimat ide pokok.

3. Murid berdiskusi singkat berpasangan mengenai dugaan ide pokok paragraf tersebut (1-2 menit).

MENGAPLIKASIKAN (Data Collection & Data Processing) - 25 Menit

1. Murid berkelompok (3-4 orang) menerima satu dari tiga paragraf “The Legend of Telaga Lestari” (Orientation, Complication, atau Resolution) secara bergilir.

2. Tiap kelompok menggarisbawahi kata kunci, menulis catatan pinggir, lalu menentukan ide pokok paragrafnya menggunakan 3 Magic Questions. (Penalaran Kritis, Kolaborasi)

3. Sebelum waktu habis, tiap anggota berbagi minimal satu kata kunci/catatan (Round Robin).

MEREFLEKSI (Verification & Generalization) - 15 Menit

1. Perwakilan tiap kelompok memaparkan ide pokok paragrafnya (diusahakan wakil dari ketiga paragraf).

2. Guru dan murid mencocokkan dengan kunci ide pokok resmi.

3. Murid menyimpulkan tiga langkah note-taking: Read and Search → Underline and Note → Conclude (generalization).

4. Murid menulis refleksi singkat: bagaimana note-taking membantu memahami cerita lebih baik. (Kemandirian)
Kegiatan Penutup (5 Menit)
Prinsip: Bermakna, Menggembirakan

1. Murid bersama Guru menyimpulkan capaian TP 1.1 secara keseluruhan (struktur, ciri legenda, kosakata, ide pokok).

2. Guru memberikan umpan balik dan apresiasi menyeluruh.

3. Guru menyampaikan pengantar TP 1.2 (analisis unsur kebahasaan: action verbs, simple past tense, sequence words) yang akan dimulai Pertemuan 5, dengan penguatan grammar dasar terlebih dahulu.

4. Guru menutup pembelajaran dengan doa dan salam: “Amazing work today, idea hunters! See you in our next lesson.”

LAMPIRAN 2 - ASESMEN AWAL PEMBELAJARAN (DIAGNOSTIK)

Jenis: Asesmen Formatif-Diagnostik | Bentuk: 6 soal pilihan ganda dan 4 soal esai singkat | Teknik: dikerjakan secara tertulis sebelum Pertemuan 1 tanpa perangkat digital.

Bagian A - Pilihan Ganda (Lingkari salah satu jawaban yang paling tepat)

1. A legend usually begins with a part that introduces the characters and setting. This part is called the ______.
a. resolution b. orientation c. complication d. reiteration

2. Which of the following words most likely appears in a story about the environment?
a. deforestation b. debate c. election d. calculation

3. The part of a story where a problem or conflict appears is called the ______.
a. orientation b. complication c. resolution d. thesis

4. “Once upon a time, in a small village near a great forest...” This sentence most likely belongs to the ______ part of a legend.
a. complication b. resolution c. orientation d. argument

5. A legend is a type of ______ text that often explains the origin of a place, animal, or natural phenomenon.
a. narrative b. expository c. procedural d. persuasive

6. Which of the following is NOT typically a characteristic of a legend?
a. magical or supernatural elements b. explains the origin of something real c. written strictly as a scientific report d. carries a moral message

Bagian B - Esai Singkat

1. In your own words, explain what a “legend” is.

2. Mention one example of a legend (in Bahasa Indonesia or English) that you have heard before, and briefly explain what it is about.

MATERI PERTEMUAN 1: Struktur Generik Teks Naratif atau Legenda

A. What Is a Legend?

A legend is a type of narrative text. People tell it from one generation to the next. A legend usually explains how a place, an animal, or a natural thing came to be. Legends often mix real history with imagination or magic. They also carry a moral message for the listener or reader. A legend is almost always connected to something real that still exists today, such as a lake, a mountain, or an island. This is why people find legends believable, even though they contain magical parts.

B. Generic Structure of a Legend (Struktur Generik)

Every legend, like other narrative texts, follows three main parts. Knowing this structure helps readers and listeners guess what information comes next. It also helps writers organise their own stories.

Structure Function Typical Language Signal
Orientation Introduces the characters, the setting (time and place), and the background of the story. This is where we meet the main character and learn about their normal life before anything goes wrong. Once upon a time... / A long time ago, in a village near... / There lived a...
Complication Presents a problem or an unusual event. This event changes the calm situation from the orientation. This is the main part of the story's tension. Something changes, usually for the worse. One day... / Suddenly... / However, a terrible drought came... / Things changed when...
Resolution Explains how the problem is solved. In most legends, this part also explains the real origin of a place, an animal, or a natural thing. This is also where the moral lesson becomes clear. Finally... / From that day on... / Since then, the lake has been called... / Ever since that day...

Tip for students: when you listen to a legend for the first time, focus on three things only. WHO the story is about (Orientation). WHAT problem appears (Complication). HOW the story ends (Resolution). You do not need to remember every small detail on the first listen.

C. Sample Text: "The Legend of the Weeping Lake" (dipakai Pertemuan 1 sampai 3)

Orientation: A long time ago, in a small village near a thick forest, there lived a kind old woman named Mai Rimba. She took care of the forest and its animals like they were her own family. Every animal in the forest loved and trusted her.

Complication: One day, a group of greedy loggers came to the village. They wanted to cut down the whole forest to sell the wood. Mai Rimba asked them to stop. She warned them that the forest protected the village from floods. The loggers laughed at her and kept cutting the trees. Soon, heavy rain came. Without the trees to hold the soil, a terrible flood hit the village.

Resolution: Mai Rimba cried for the lost forest and the suffering of her village. The legend says her tears were so many and so real that they formed a wide, deep lake at the edge of the forest. Since that day, the villagers have called it The Weeping Lake. They also protect the trees around it, so this sad event never happens again.

MATERI PERTEMUAN 2: Ciri-Ciri Legenda

Characteristics of a Legend (Ciri-Ciri Legenda)

Beyond the three-part structure, a legend has special features. These features make a legend different from other kinds of stories, such as a news report or a fairy tale. Below are the five characteristics used in the Pertemuan 2 checklist. Each one is explained with why it matters.

Characteristic Explanation
Magical or supernatural elements Legends usually include something that cannot happen in real life. Examples: tears becoming a lake, a guardian spirit, or an animal that can talk. This part makes the story easy to remember and different from a fact report.
Explains the origin of something real Almost every legend answers this question: why does this place, animal, or thing exist, or have this name? This is the main purpose of a legend. It is a story people made to explain something real.
Set in a specific, real location Unlike "Once upon a time, in a faraway kingdom" (a fairy tale), legends are tied to real, named places. This could be a village, a lake, or a mountain that listeners could actually visit.
Delivers a moral value or lesson Legends are also a teaching tool. People tell them to pass down values, such as respect for nature, honesty, or humility, to the next generation.
Oral tradition (passed down by word of mouth) People told legends aloud for many years before anyone wrote them down. This is why legends often use phrases like "it is said that..." or "according to the elders...".

A short comparison: a news report does NOT have the five characteristics above. It has no magical element, it does not explain an origin in a magical way, and it usually does not carry a clear moral message. This comparison is the stimulus used at the start of Pertemuan 2.

MATERI PERTEMUAN 3: Kosakata Tematik Lingkungan

Environmental Vocabulary Glossary

The strategy used in Pertemuan 3 is called Text Evidence Matching. Instead of memorising dictionary definitions, students find the SENTENCE in the text that shows the meaning of a word. This strategy trains students to understand a word from context. This is an important skill, because in real texts and real exams, a new word rarely comes with its definition right beside it.

Term Definition Example / Evidence
Ecosystem A community of living things that interact with their environment. "...a balanced ecosystem, home to many animals..." (dari teks Telaga Lestari, dipakai sebagai contoh tambahan)
Habitat The natural home or environment of a plant or an animal. "...living safely in their natural habitat."
Deforestation The action of clearing a wide area of trees, usually for wood or land. "They wanted to cut down the whole forest to sell the wood."
Extinction The state when a type of animal or plant no longer exists anywhere in the world. Tidak tertulis langsung di teks. Murid menyimpulkannya (infer) dari pengulangan kerusakan habitat (pertanyaan berpikir kritis).
Conservation The protection and careful management of the environment and natural resources. "...practiced conservation by planting trees, protecting wildlife..."

Word family note: deforestation (noun), to deforest (verb), deforested (adjective). Conservation (noun), to conserve (verb), a conservationist (a person who does this work). Teaching these word families helps students recognise the same word in a different form when they read other texts.

MATERI PERTEMUAN 4: Menemukan Ide Pokok (Note-Taking)

D. Sample Text: "The Legend of Telaga Lestari" (teks aktif mulai Pertemuan 4 dan seterusnya)

Orientation: Long ago, there was a hidden lake in Indonesia called Telaga Lestari. The lake was surrounded by a beautiful forest with a balanced ecosystem. It was the natural habitat of many animals. The villagers believed that a mysterious forest guardian watched over the land and protected nature.

Complication: One day, the villagers became greedy and started cutting down many trees to build a larger settlement. Their deforestation damaged the forest, polluted the lake, and forced many animals to leave their homes. Some rare animals were even close to extinction. The villagers ignored the guardian's warning until a great storm came with strong winds and heavy rain. The storm destroyed their settlement and made them realise that their actions had harmed the balance of nature.

Resolution: After the disaster, the villagers said sorry and worked together to restore the forest and lake. They practiced conservation by planting trees, protecting wildlife, and keeping the environment clean. Slowly, the forest became healthy again, and the animals returned to their habitat. Since then, the lake has been called Telaga Lestari, meaning a place that stays preserved. The story of the forest guardian has been passed down from generation to generation as a reminder that humans must respect and protect nature.

The Three Magic Questions (Finding the Main Idea)

The note-taking technique taught in Pertemuan 4 has three steps, done in order.

Step What to Do Applied to Telaga Lestari: Orientation
1. Read and Search Read the paragraph. Underline the keywords. These are usually the most important nouns and verbs. hidden lake, Telaga Lestari, balanced ecosystem, natural habitat, forest guardian
2. Underline and Note Answer the three magic questions: (1) WHO or WHAT is this about? (2) WHAT HAPPENED, or WHAT DID THEY DO? (3) WHAT WAS THE RESULT? Q1: Telaga Lestari and the forest. Q2: had a balanced ecosystem and a guardian. Q3: everything was peaceful.
3. Conclude Combine the three answers into ONE main idea sentence. Telaga Lestari was a hidden lake with a balanced ecosystem, home to many animals, and watched over by a forest guardian.

MATERI PERTEMUAN 5: Penguatan Grammar Dasar - Simple Past Tense

A. Simple Past Tense: Regular and Irregular Verbs

We use the simple past tense to talk about actions that finished in the past. This is exactly what legends do, because a legend describes events that already happened, a long time ago.

Verb Type Rule Examples
Regular Verbs Add -ed to the base form. live to lived / cry to cried / want to wanted / beg to begged
Irregular Verbs The verb form changes completely. Students must memorise each one. go to went / see to saw / come to came / take to took

Common spelling rules for regular verbs: (1) most verbs simply add -ed (walk to walked). (2) verbs ending in -e only add -d (live to lived). (3) verbs ending in consonant + y change y to i and add -ed (worry to worried, but NOT play to plaied, since play ends in a vowel + y, so it becomes played). (4) short verbs ending in one vowel + one consonant often double the last letter (stop to stopped, plan to planned).

Grammar Wall reference for Pertemuan 5 to 8 (kept on the board throughout): live-lived-lived, cry-cried-cried, want-wanted-wanted, beg-begged-begged, go-went-gone, see-saw-seen, come-came-come, take-took-taken.

B. Action Verbs in "The Legend of Telaga Lestari"

An action verb is a verb that shows a physical or mental action. Below is the full text again, with the main action verbs marked in the list beneath it, for teacher reference during Pertemuan 6.

Orientation: Long ago, there was a hidden lake in Indonesia called Telaga Lestari. The lake was surrounded by a beautiful forest with a balanced ecosystem. It was the natural habitat of many animals, and the villagers believed that a mysterious forest guardian watched over the land and protected nature.

Complication: One day, the villagers became greedy and started cutting down many trees to build a larger settlement. Their deforestation damaged the forest, polluted the lake, and forced many animals to leave their homes. Some rare animals were even close to extinction. The villagers ignored the guardian's warning until a great storm came with strong winds and heavy rain. The storm destroyed their settlement and made them realize that their actions had harmed the balance of nature.

Resolution: After the disaster, the villagers apologized and worked together to restore the forest and lake. They practiced conservation by planting trees, protecting wildlife, and keeping the environment clean. Slowly, the forest became healthy again, and the animals returned to their habitat. Since then, the lake has been called Telaga Lestari, meaning a place that remains preserved. The story of the forest guardian has been passed down from generation to generation as a reminder that humans must respect and protect nature.

C. The Legend of Pulau Setokok (Batam), Summary

Orientation: A long time ago, near the coast of Batam, there was a small island where fishermen lived peacefully with the sea. The island's forest and mangroves protected the shore and gave shelter to many fish and birds.

Complication: One season, some fishermen began using explosives to catch fish quickly. This destroyed the coral reefs around the island. The fish disappeared, and the mangroves began to die because the reef could no longer protect them from strong waves.

Resolution: The village elders gathered the fishermen and reminded them of their ancestors' promise to protect the sea. They stopped the harmful practice and planted new mangroves. Over time, the fish returned. The island, later called Pulau Setokok, became known as a place that stood firm (“setokok”) in protecting its natural balance.

Action verbs found in this text: lived, protected, gave, began, destroyed, disappeared, began (die), gathered, reminded, stopped, planted, returned, became. Guru dapat memakai daftar ini sebagai kunci jawaban Part B pada LKPD Pertemuan 6.

MATERI PERTEMUAN 7: Sequence Words

D. Sequence Words

Sequence words help a reader follow the order of events in a story. Without them, a story can feel confusing, even if every sentence is grammatically correct.

Sequence Word Function Example
First / At first Marks the beginning of a sequence. First, the villagers lived peacefully beside the lake and its forest.
Then / Next / After that Marks the event that follows. Then, the villagers became greedy and started cutting down many trees.
Suddenly Marks a surprising or unexpected event. Suddenly, a great storm came with strong winds and heavy rain.
Finally / From that day on Marks the result or the ending of the story. Finally, the lake was named Telaga Lestari, meaning a place that remains preserved.

MATERI PERTEMUAN 9: Mengevaluasi Efektivitas Teks Naratif

A. Criteria for Evaluating a Narrative Text

To evaluate a text means to judge how well it works, not just to summarise what it says. A good evaluation always gives a reason, supported by evidence from the text. Below are four criteria students use in Pertemuan 9.

Criterion Guiding Question
Clarity of Structure Are orientation, complication, and resolution easy to identify?
Accuracy of Language Are action verbs, simple past tense, and sequence words used correctly?
Clarity of Message Does the story deliver its moral or environmental message clearly?
Believability Do the characters' actions and the story's outcome feel logical inside the story world?

Sample evaluation (modelled by the teacher, using Telaga Lestari): “The structure is very clear. I can easily find the orientation (the peaceful lake), the complication (the villagers cutting trees), and the resolution (the villagers restoring the forest). I rate this criterion 4 out of 4, because each part has clear language signals such as 'One day' and 'After the disaster'.” Guru dapat mencontohkan pola argumen seperti ini di depan kelas sebelum murid mengerjakan LKPD secara mandiri.

MATERI PERTEMUAN 10: Refleksi Text-to-World Connection

B. What Is a Text-to-World Connection?

A text-to-world connection is a link between something that happens in a story and something that happens in real life or the wider world. For example, the deforestation in The Legend of Telaga Lestari connects to real deforestation issues happening in Indonesia and around the world today. Making this kind of connection helps students see that a legend is not just an old story. It can still teach a lesson that matters right now.

Three question starters students can use to build their own text-to-world connections:

- This part of the story reminds me of... (name a real place, event, or habit)

- In real life, this problem happens when... (explain a real cause)

- If people in real life acted like the villagers in the resolution, then... (explain a possible real result)

MATERI PERTEMUAN 11-12: Pengambilan Gambar dan Penyusunan Struktur

A. Four-Stage Structure for the Poster Project

The Unit 1 poster project uses a structure with four stages, not just three. This is because a picture story needs a clear "peak moment" (Climax) before the problem is solved.

Stage What It Shows
1. Orientation Introduces the characters, the setting, and the school's normal environmental condition or habit.
2. Complication Shows the environmental problem that appears, for example littering, dying plants, or wasted water.
3. Climax / Evaluation Shows the peak of the problem, or its biggest impact so far.
4. Resolution Shows how the problem is solved and states the Moral Lesson at the end.

Tip for choosing pictures (Pertemuan 11): a good set of pictures usually shows a clear "before and after" feeling. Take at least one picture for each stage, so your group has enough material to build a complete story in Pertemuan 12.

MATERI PERTEMUAN 13-14: Menulis Narasi dan Menyiapkan Presentasi

B. Narration Sentence Starters for the Poster

Students can use these sentence starters to help write the narration under each picture. Each starter already includes the correct simple past tense form.

Stage Sentence Starter
Orientation In our school, students usually...
Complication One day, we noticed that...
Climax / Evaluation The problem became worse when...
Resolution Finally, we decided to... / From that day on, we...

Reminder: the narration must use the three language features from TP 1.2: action verbs, simple past tense, and sequence words (first, then, after that, finally). The Moral Lesson at the bottom of the poster can use a simple present tense sentence, since a moral lesson is a general truth, not a single past event. Example: "We must always take care of our school environment."

MATERI PERTEMUAN 15-16: Presentasi

C. Presentation Sentence Frames

- Opening: "Good morning, everyone. Our story is about..."

- Body: "First, ... Then, ... After that, ... Finally, ..."

- Closing: "The moral lesson of our story is... Thank you for listening. Do you have any questions?"

Tips for a confident presentation: speak a little slower than normal conversation speed, look at the audience (not only at the poster), and practice your part at least once before Pertemuan 15. If a listener asks a question you do not fully understand, it is okay to say: "Can you repeat the question, please?" This is a normal and polite thing to say, even for fluent speakers.

Reading Stimulus for Paket B — “The Song of the Green Valley”

High among the hills, there was a fertile valley where a girl named Wulan lived with her family. The valley had a thriving ecosystem: fruit trees, singing birds, and clean streams that gave every creature a safe habitat. One year, farmers from another region began clearing large areas of forest for quick profit, and they used excessive chemical fertiliser on the exposed soil. Deforestation spread across the hillside, and the once-clean streams turned murky. Birds and small animals started losing their habitat, and some species were feared close to extinction. One evening, dark clouds gathered, and the wind carried a strange, mournful song through the valley — a warning no one had heard before. Moments later, a small landslide blocked the path the farmers used to bring their machines, stopping their work before greater damage was done. Frightened and humbled, the farmers apologised and joined the villagers in a long conservation effort. Slowly, the trees grew back and the birds returned. From then on, people called it “Lembah Bernyanyi” — the Singing Valley — for the song that had once saved it.
